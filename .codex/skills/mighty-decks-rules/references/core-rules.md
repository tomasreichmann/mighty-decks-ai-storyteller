## Common Drift Checks

- Maximum Injury causes **Taken Out**, not Dying. Death Draws and Sacrifice belong to the optional lethal rules.
- A Partial Success is `+1 Effect` and does not automatically create a Complication card.
- A Fumble most likely means the attempted action fails. The Storyteller may instead rule a partial success with a serious Complication when that better fits the fiction.
- A default Complication applies `-1 Effect` the next time it is relevant, then is discarded. The Storyteller may define another fitting effect or removal condition.
- Check Catastrophe only after the current Action or Defense resolves and the replacement card is drawn. It triggers on 3+ Fumbles or when the entire hand is Fumbles; discard only the triggering Fumbles and refill to the current hand size.
- A player may use one Outcome for their Action and any number of legal Defenses in a round. Chaos cannot defend; a defensive Fumble normally adds no protection without increasing the incoming Effect.
- Agree on a simple player order before the Adventure. An NPC Actor acts after the player its card is placed in front of and uses fixed Effects, not an Outcome hand.
- Scene Stunts may be offered face up, up to the player count. The first player to meet a displayed requirement gains that Stunt immediately for the rest of the Adventure.
- Low Tension normally clears all Injury and Distress only when the characters have a reasonable opportunity to rest and recover; an Adventure may explicitly override this.

When a summary, card, example, or prototype disagrees with these checks, verify the exact rulebook wording and correct the stale source rather than blending both versions.
