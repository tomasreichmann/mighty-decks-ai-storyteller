# Player Summary
![](/api/adventure-artifacts/exiles-of-the-hungry-void-title-36059ffa4f9afb3dc1d4.jpg)
The Empire has thrown you and a handful of other unwanted survivors beyond the last safe border, aboard a half-dead corvette that barely deserves to be called a ship.
You start as strangers with mismatched talents, no support, and no way back. To survive the Hungry Void, you will need to get the wreck moving, make hard calls under pressure, and become a real crew before the Void decides you are easy prey.
