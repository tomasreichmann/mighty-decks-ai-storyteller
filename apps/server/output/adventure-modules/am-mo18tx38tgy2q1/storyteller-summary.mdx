## Premise

The Empire has thrown a handful of exiles into the Hungry Void aboard a crippled corvette and made one thing clear: there is no going back.

## Campaign Intent

The opening should establish the crew through action, not exposition. Push the players to coordinate quickly, claim ship roles, and solve a few immediate problems so they feel the thrill of becoming a functioning crew. Once they start to believe escape is possible, bring in the <GameCard type="ActorCard" slug="void-horror" /> and turn their fragile progress into a desperate shared test.
