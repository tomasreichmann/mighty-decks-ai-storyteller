# Missile Stockpile

💡 This encounter is a quick one-round threat → resolution encounter. A missile battery launches 2x player count of different rockets immediately when the space-ship jumps in. Players can each try to destroy/hack/evade the rockets. Each missile that is not dealt with strikes a random system on the ship. The types of rockets are Incendiary, Breach, EMP, Toxic.

*The stars wrench themselves back into familiar points of light as you exit the jump. Before the ship even settles, proximity alarms scream to life. A nearby asteroid, disguised as inert rock, slides open to reveal an automated missile battery. It fires a volley of missiles. **"Incoming! Multiple warheads!"** says the ship's machine spirit dryly.*

*A number of different missiles (equal to **twice the number of crew members**) streaks toward your ship. They are a mixed bag of different technology and payloads different payload, a different flavor of disaster.*

* **Incendiary Missile:** Upon impact, starts a raging **Fire** in a random room it struck. Anyone in that room suffers **1 Burning** every turn until repaired.
* **Breach Missile:** Causes a hull breach and vents the air from the room. Anyone in that room suffers **1 Freezing** every turn until repaired.
* **EMP Missile:** Disables a random system, making it **Inoperable** for a round making the energy wasted.
* **Toxic Missile:** Detonates a chemical payload, filling a random room with toxic gas. Anyone in that room takes **1 Distress** every turn until vented.

***

### Brace for Impact!

*You have seconds to act. Each crew member can try to intercept some missiles.*

🎭 **How do you want to deal with the missiles?**

* **Destroy it:** Target a missile with the ship's weapons. Each Effect destroys one missile.
* **Hack it:** Splice into the missile's guidance system, turning it into a dud or making it veer off course.
  * **Success:** You gain control of the missile.
  * **Partial success:** The missile drifts harmlessly into space.
  * **Failure:** On a failure, the counter-intrusion locks you out.
* **Evade it:** Each effect allows you to evade one missile.
* Actor reference: Artillery Fire / Ranger / Tough

### Resolution

*The next few seconds are a blur of frantic activity, explosions, and near misses.*

* For each **Effect**, one missile is neutralized.
* The rest of the missiles strike your ship.
  * 💡 For each missile that hits, roll a D4 to find out what type it is. Then roll a die to determine which system is struck. Apply the missile's effect.\_

*As quickly as it began, the assault is over. the echoes of explosions still ringing in your ears and the smell of ozone and smoke filling the corridors.*

* The automated battery, having expended its loaded missiles, retracts back into the asteroid.
* You find a door on the other side of the asteroid, where a supply of missile parts are stored. There is a data-shard with missile schematics, but the machine spirit seems to only want to part with a single missile before it destroys the data-shard.
  🏺 Which missile design do you want to keep?
  * **Incendiary Missile**
  * **Breach Missile**
  * **EMP Missile**
  * **Toxic Missile**
